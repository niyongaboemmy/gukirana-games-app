# Credits for the review images in this folder (P2-AVA-13)

The review JPGs here are renders of our game (the dev-only human lab). The v9 people in them are adaptations of
these 3D scans, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). Changes: cleaned, straightened, re-rigged, re-animated with CMU
motion capture, decimated, re-baked, re-lit. The scans themselves are not in this repository.

- "Woman" by in3D (<https://sketchfab.com/in3D_io>), <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/woman-4188f7fc248c4f20a6e00744ad394f9e>, CC BY 4.0.
- "Dima" by in3D (<https://sketchfab.com/in3D_io>), <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dima-3a606acdfbe04478b0ab8dfe0f1de978>, CC BY 4.0.
- "Full body" by fwild (<https://sketchfab.com/fwild>), <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/full-body-a5986a27070c465280d7419cf783de31>, CC BY 4.0.

Motion: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, <http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu> ("The data used in this project was
obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."), via the BVH mirror
<https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap>.
