# Avatar reference: Rwandan faces and hair (art direction)

Written reference for anyone working on avatar faces, heads and hair. It describes what "realistic" means for the
people of Gukirana Playground (Musanze, Rwanda). It is a guide for proportions, colour and styling, not a template:
real people vary widely, so variety matters more than any single "typical" face. Avoid caricature and stereotypes.
Before launch, have the result reviewed by Rwandan players and staff (DESIGN-SPEC §5.2 cultural review).

Photo references: use only properly licensed sources (Wikimedia Commons with a free licence, stock sites whose licence
allows reference use, or an own photoshoot with consent) and keep them as internal references. Never ship a real
person's photo or likeness in the app.

## 1. Skin

- Range: warm light brown to very deep brown, with undertones from warm golden/red to neutral and cool. Offer at
  least 8–10 tones and make sure the deepest tones still show form (cheekbones, nose, lips) under every lighting setup.
- Indicative albedo palette (sRGB, before lighting): `#c68e62` · `#b07a52` · `#9a6644` · `#875535` · `#73462b` ·
  `#603a24` · `#4f2f1e` · `#3f2618` · `#321e14` · `#28180f`.
- Skin is never one flat colour: slightly warmer/redder on cheeks, nose and ears; slightly darker around the eyes,
  the upper lip and the jaw line; a touch lighter on the forehead and cheekbones. Palms and soles are much lighter.
- Shine: soft, broad and low (forehead, nose bridge, cheekbones). Never plastic or glossy. Deep skin needs a little
  more visible sheen than light skin to read its shape; keep it matte overall (no glare).
- Detail: fine pores, faint creases under the eyes and at the mouth corners, and natural unevenness. Freckles are
  uncommon; small beauty marks are fine.

## 2. Faces

- Wide natural variety: oval, long and round faces; high or soft cheekbones; narrow to broad noses with a low to
  medium bridge; full lips are common but not universal; strong or soft jaws. Do not make everyone the same.
- Eyes: dark brown to near-black irises (lighter brown occasionally); the sclera is warm off-white, never pure white;
  slight darkening of the lids. Lashes are dark; brows are natural, dark, often fairly full with soft hair-like edges.
- Lips: the natural colour is a darker or rosier version of the skin, often with a darker upper lip and a slightly
  lighter, more pink-brown lower lip. Not pink lipstick by default. Matte to softly moist, never glossy by default.
- Expression: relaxed and friendly by default (lips closed or barely parted, no frown, no stare).

## 3. Men's hair

- Most common: very short tight coils close to the scalp (a low cut / "Caesar"), often with a crisp hairline;
  fades and tapers (skin or very short at the sides, a little longer on top); buzz cuts; fully shaved heads.
- Also: 360 waves, a short high-top, twists, short locs; older men are often close-cropped with some grey.
- Hairline: natural and clean, slightly rounded at the temples, sideburns trimmed short; no hair on the ears or
  temple skin. A fade must be a smooth gradient from skin to hair, not a painted line.
- Texture: tightly coiled (type 4), matte with a soft satin sheen; reads as fine texture, not a smooth helmet.
- Facial hair: clean-shaven is the most common; short neat beards, goatees and moustaches are common; edges are soft
  and follow the jaw and lip line, with skin showing through at the edges. Stubble is fine short hairs, not a smudge.

## 4. Women's hair

- Very common: braids (box braids, knotless braids, long or shoulder-length), cornrows (straight back or patterned),
  twists, a short natural afro (TWA) or a full afro, puffs and buns, relaxed or straightened hair, and wigs/weaves.
- Headwraps (igitambaro) are common, from everyday simple wraps to tall, pleated festive wraps; the fabric has real
  folds and thickness, sits snugly on the hairline (no gap, no jagged edge) and ties at the back or the crown.
- Braids and locs have real volume and weight: they hang from the scalp with gravity, move with the head and sit
  in rows with visible parting. Afros have a soft, irregular outline, not a perfect sphere.
- Edges: soft baby hairs at the hairline are a styling choice (often neatly laid), not paint bleeding onto the skin.

## 5. Traditional and heritage styles (for festive / cultural options)

- Amasunzu: the historic Rwandan crescent-ridge hairstyle (worn by men and unmarried women), with shaped ridges of
  hair. Treat it respectfully as a heritage option and get it reviewed.
- Ingofero / kufi-style caps for men; imishanana (the traditional draped attire) for women at ceremonies, often
  with an elegant headwrap or neatly styled hair.

## 6. Caps and hats, worn correctly

- A baseball cap sits on the skull, not on top of the hair: the band rests around the forehead (just above the
  brows), the temples and the back of the head; the brim has thickness and a natural curve; the ears stay outside.
  Short hair shows a little below the band at the sides and the nape; nothing pokes through the crown.
- Beanies hug the head and fold over at the band; bucket hats have a soft drooping brim; kufi caps sit straight and
  close on the crown.

## 7. Common mistakes to avoid (seen in our screenshots)

- Hair colour painted onto the temple skin, the sideburn area or the ears.
- Short hair as a smooth painted cap with a hard, ragged hairline.
- A jagged, stepped edge where a headwrap or cap meets the forehead; wraps that sit high and boxy on the head.
- Beards and goatees as flat, patchy dark shapes; stubble as a grey smear.
- Pink or glossy lips by default; a blue sheen on black hair from the lighting.
- Everyone with the same face, the same hairstyle and the same skin tone.
