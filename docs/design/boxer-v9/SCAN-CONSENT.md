# Likeness consent: 3D scan for Gukirana Playground (template)

This is a template. Print it, or copy it into a private document, and fill it in with the person before the scan.
**The signed copy is stored privately (a locked drawer, or a private folder with the scan backup). It is never
committed to the code repository, never uploaded with the game files and never shared publicly.** Only the blank
template lives in the repository. Give the person a copy of what they signed.

If the person prefers, read it to them in Kinyarwanda, French or Swahili, and note the language below.

---

## What this is about

Gukirana Playground is a 3D social skill-games campus made in Musanze, Rwanda. We would like to take about 150 to 200
photos of you with a phone and turn them into a 3D model of your body and face (a "scan"). We will use that model as
a boxer character in the game.

Please read each point. Ask any question before you sign. You do not have to agree, and saying no has no consequences
for you.

## 1. I am an adult

- [ ] I confirm that I am **18 years old or older**. (We may ask to see an ID. We do not keep a copy of it.)

## 2. How my likeness will be used

- [ ] I agree that my 3D scan, and characters made from it (my face, body shape, skin and hair, possibly changed in
      colour, size, clothing or hair), may be used **in the Gukirana Playground game and its promotion** (screenshots,
      videos, store pages).
- [ ] I understand that the game includes **boxing matches between players, including rooms where players play for
      real money stakes** ("cash-stakes rooms"), and that my likeness may appear as a boxer in those rooms. My character
      may win, lose, be hit or be knocked down in the game.
- [ ] I understand that the game is published on the internet, so people anywhere can see my likeness, and that game
      files downloaded to players' devices could be copied by someone without permission, even though we keep the scan
      itself private.
- [ ] I understand that my likeness will **not** be used to show me endorsing a product, a political party or a
      religion, and will not be used in sexual or degrading content.

## 3. No AI training

- [ ] Gukirana will **not use my scan, photos or likeness to train or test artificial intelligence or machine learning
      systems**, and will not sell or give them to anyone for that purpose.

## 4. My right to withdraw

- [ ] I may **withdraw my consent at any time** by telling Gukirana (contact below) in writing or by message.
- [ ] After I withdraw, Gukirana will **not use my likeness in any future build or update** of the game, and will
      delete my raw scan and photos within 30 days, except for this signed form, which is kept as a record.
- [ ] I understand that copies already published before I withdrew (for example a version of the game already on
      players' devices, or a video already posted) may take some time to replace or may not be fully removable.
